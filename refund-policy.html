MODWAI Refund Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2025

At MODWAI, we strive to provide exceptional software and service. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be requested.

1. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be requested within 7 days of the original purchase date under the following conditions:

  • Technical issues that prevent the application from functioning as described, and our support team is unable to resolve them.
  • The product does not meet the specifications described on our website.
  • Accidental duplicate purchases.

2. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be granted in the following cases:

  • After the 7-day refund period has expired.
  • If the User violated the Terms of Service.
  • If the subscription was already used for production database monitoring.
  • Change of mind after successful use of the application.
  • Lack of Oracle licenses required to use certain features (this is User's responsibility).
  • Issues caused by User misconfiguration or unauthorized database access.

3. Subscription Cancellation

Users may cancel their subscription at any time:

  • Monthly subscriptions: No refund for the current billing period; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Annual subscriptions: Refunds available only within the first 7 days. After 7 days, access continues until the end of the subscription period, but no refund is provided.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Contact our support team at: hello@modwai.eu
  2. Include your order number and reason for the refund request.
  3. Our team will review your request within 5 business days.

5. Refund Processing

If your refund is approved:

  • Refunds are processed through Paddle.com Market Ltd (our Merchant of Record).
  • The refund will be issued to the original payment method.
  • Processing time: 5-10 business days depending on your payment provider.

6. Free Trial

If we offer a free trial period in the future:

  • No charges apply during the trial.
  • You may cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid being charged.
  • No refunds are needed for cancelled trials.

7. Exceptional Circumstances

In cases of exceptional circumstances (e.g., serious technical failures, service unavailability), we may grant refunds outside the standard policy at our discretion.

8. Contact

For refund inquiries, please contact: hello@modwai.eu

All refund requests are handled by our support team in cooperation with Paddle.com Market Ltd.
